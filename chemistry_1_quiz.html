Study tips

Study tip: Group related ions together: nitrate/nitrite, sulfate/sulfite, phosphate/phosphite, chlorate family, and hydrogen variants like bicarbonate and bisulfate.

Naming tip: -ate usually has more oxygen than -ite. Prefixes like per- mean one more oxygen, and hypo- means one fewer.
