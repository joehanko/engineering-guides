Stress and Strain

Engineering stressσ = F/A₀
Engineering strainε = ΔL/L₀
Shear stressτ = V/A
Shear strainγ ≈ Δx/h
Hooke’s lawσ = Eε
Poisson’s ratioν = −εlat/εax
